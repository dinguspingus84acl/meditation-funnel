You already know "just relax" doesn't work at 11 p.m. Nobody failed at relaxing. The advice was backwards: "clear your mind" asks your mind to do the one thing it can't do on command.

Your mind doesn't have an off switch. Your body does.

Questions

I've tried meditation apps. They never work for me.

You're right to be skeptical. Those apps asked your mind to go quiet first, which is the one thing a busy mind can't do on command. This starts with the body instead. You'll try it for 10 minutes, live, and judge for yourself.

Do I have to be on camera or talk?

No. Camera is optional and you never have to speak. You can ask a question in the chat if you want to.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

It's free, with no card required. Why free? Feeling the practice live is the fairest way to decide whether you want to learn the full version. At the end Shalin explains the optional paid SKY Breath Meditation Part 1 course, what it includes and what it costs. If it's not for you, skip it: the free session stands on its own.

Is this religious?

No beliefs required. It's a guided breathing and awareness practice you do sitting comfortably, and you don't need any particular faith or background to take part.

Can I just watch a replay?

The 10-minute practice is guided live and responds to the room. That's the part a recording can't give you, so plan to be there live. If your time stops working, switch to another live session: {{rebook_link}}. Your answers stay saved on this device.

Will this work for my situation? (shift work, pain, can't sit on the floor)

You do the practice sitting in any chair or on a couch, and nothing involves the floor. It isn't a treatment and doesn't replace medical care, so if you have a health condition, check with your doctor about breathing exercises. The fairest test is the one you run yourself: rate your mind 1–10 before class and again right after the practice.

What if I nod off during the practice?

Sit up on a couch or chair rather than lying in bed, and you'll be able to follow along. If you drift for a moment, Shalin's voice brings you back. With your camera off, nobody sees either way.

What if my kids or partner interrupt?

Tell them ahead of time: "I'm offline for 75 minutes" (it's on the checklist above). If you get pulled away, tap your join link again to rejoin.
